📈 Elevation Profile — Walked Order

🔴 Hole Punch — Out & BackClimb 2.4 mi / +1,161 ft to the summit at 8,301 ft — the highest point on the route — then descend the same path back to Water Station D1. Happens early Day 1.
💪 Hardest Single SegmentLow Water Station → Water Station D3: 5.5 mi / +1,716 ft. Longest sustained climb after the valley loop. Start it well fueled and watered.
↩ Crab Flats Figure-8The route descends through Crab Flats, continues down to the low Water Station, then loops back up through both on the way out. Adds ~2.3 mi in the lowest, hottest zone.
💧 Critical Water GapLow Water Station → Water Station D3 is 5.5 miles of climbing with no water source. Carry full capacity before starting this climb.

🧠 Strategic Notes by Day

Day 1 — Summit early, then descendStart → Water D1 (3.3 mi) → Hole Punch summit at 8,301 ft (2.4 mi) → back to Water D1 → Green Valley camp at 14.1 mi cumulative. Get Hole Punch done early and don't burn out on the descent.
Day 2 — Valley figure-8 loopGreen Valley → Crab Flats (5.2 mi) → Water D2 → low Water Station → back through Crab Flats → low Water Station again. Expect to feel like you're going in circles — you are. Stay on route, save legs for the climb out.
Day 3 — Biggest climb outAfter the second pass through the low Water Station, the 5.5 mi / +1,716 ft climb to Water Station D3 is the hardest sustained effort. Carry full water. Then Hanna Flats camp at 39.9 mi.
Days 4–5 — High plateau finishAbove 7,000 ft from Hanna Flats all the way to the finish. Cooler and more exposed — keep midlayer accessible. The final descent to Meadows Edge (5.5 mi / -518 ft) is the most comfortable segment on the route.
